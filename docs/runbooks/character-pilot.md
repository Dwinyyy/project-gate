## Stop conditions and recovery

Stop before managed changes if review or any local/CI check is incomplete; the exact SHA differs across review, CI, Vercel, or Render; preflight is stale or unexpected; migration receipt/checksum or grant read-back differs; public access appears; the switch or grants are already on unexpectedly; or an anonymous boundary has the wrong status, request ID, cache policy, or revision. After the private pilot is ready, freeze or turn off the switch if its grant, actor eligibility, cooldown, living-slot, migration, revision, or aggregate-count preconditions unexpectedly change or fail.

If the owner journey is unavailable, uncertain, or inconsistent after the pilot has been enabled, preserve the authorized single-player gate while the issue is investigated; freeze or turn off the switch if an unexpected state or failed safety precondition is found. Report the safe error and nonsecret request ID. Do not rerun creation with a new operation identity while the outcome is uncertain. Refresh confirmed state first and use only the UI’s explicit same-draft retry if it is offered. Never delete a character, change balances/ownership, perform financial operations, or attempt a destructive rollback. A committed migration is handled through a reviewed forward repair if needed; do not rewrite migration history.

When testing ends, turn off the global switch and keep access restricted to the designated account. Revoking a pilot grant must not delete the durable game account, character, or history. Keep public lethal play disabled.
