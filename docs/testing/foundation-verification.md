## Limitations

The hosted preview is an account-enabled foundation shell, not a playable game. The owner reported the enrolled and unenrolled recovery journeys passed on the reviewed 5c1bc5c deployment; the unenrolled handoff included registration/verification, password sign-in, refresh, recovery and explicit sign-in with the replacement password. The designated operator completed real enrollment, audited grant, AAL2 authorization and sign-out/reauthentication on the previous standalone hosted checkpoint. The reviewed recovery TOTP step-up is deployed on matching frontend/API code and the main-site operator round trip has passed. The newly approved mobile, private-entry and registration-confirmation changes still need their final integrated verification and development deployment.

Direct Supabase account-management endpoints follow provider policy independently of our UI. Email compromise still permits password recovery; protect the inbox with MFA. Global sign-out revokes refresh sessions but previously issued access tokens follow provider expiry/revocation behavior. DevTools cannot be disabled as a security boundary, and these checks cannot promise zero vulnerabilities. Characters, gameplay, economy, location and mobile packaging are not implemented. A01–A28 gameplay cases remain not-run. PostGIS belongs with geographic queries.
