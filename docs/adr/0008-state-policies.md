# ADR 0008 — Ownership, death, privacy and recovery policies

Status: accepted planning decision from the approved master blueprint; implementation is tracked separately.

## Context

Connected stateful domains cannot choose conflicting settlement or authority rules.

## Decision

Adopt product/defaults.md operational policies: ownership distinct from custody; capacity reservation and restricted overflow; fixed-destination arrival settlement; paired remote barter; irreversible valid death with estate barrier; disclosed disconnect fallback; public history preserves prior visibility.

## Consequences and alternatives

Tuning values remain in the versioned defaults register. Recovery of a proven invalid outcome needs causal reconciliation and two-person authorization; it is not ordinary resurrection. Public lethal play waits for integrated verification.
