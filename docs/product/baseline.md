# Hunter World product baseline

Version 1.0; internal source of truth. The original 40-page brief is `C:/Users/aldwi/Downloads/Hunter-World-Final-App-Concept-and-Development-Brief.pdf` (11 September 2026). [Complete transcription](brief-source.md), [32-section requirement register](requirements.md), [approved blueprint](../plans/master-blueprint.md), [decisions](../adr/README.md), [defaults](defaults.md), [acceptance](../testing/acceptance.md) and [task ledger](../tasks/ledger.md) are maintained together. Latest owner instructions take precedence.

Hunter World is a persistent modern hunter society on real geography, with civilian, hunter, crafting, merchant, explorer and guild lives. The phone is a personal System; gates open illustrated tactical encounters without camera AR. Original names, lore and assets are required. The commercial name and launch geography remain provisional.

Creation produces one unawakened identity, never a chosen combat class. Incapacitated characters occupy the living slot. Certification, class, rarity, level, profession and standing remain separate. Ordinary offline characters are sheltered; committed encounters retain disclosed risk through disconnect. Local commerce requires physical proximity, and intentional World Exchange listings provide remote commerce. Valid permanent death stays final, freezes authority and creates estate obligations. The next life is a distinct unawakened character, with no automatic inheritance.

The server owns combat, inventory, currency, eligibility, hidden awakening, grants, Blood Debt, death and cooldown. Clients show confirmed projections and issue permitted commands. Public QR tokens grant limited invitations, never transactions. Guild, market and later office power is scoped; it cannot become operator authority.

Private content, awakening conditions and evidence, account data, exact location samples and operator diagnostics stay server-only and outside player bundles, public documentation and forum templates. Public assets and DTOs are explicitly projected. This repository’s internal planning files are not player documentation.

No product feature is claimed complete by this baseline. Read the task ledger for evidenced implementation and the acceptance register for actual verification status.
