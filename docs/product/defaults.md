## Definitive operational policies

Ownership and custody are separate. Guild loans remain guild property; market custody retains vendor ownership. An instance has one current custodian; reservations prevent conflicting item, funds and capacity use. Transfers are atomic and journaled; request retries retrieve the same receipt. Offer edits invalidate acceptance. Estate settlement of an existing obligation is permitted without authorizing new actions by a dead character.

Funded delivery fixes destination, reserves capacity, retains transit custody, then releases goods and seller payment at the agreed arrival settlement. An order cannot both deliver and refund. Remote barter creates paired obligations and releases together when both sides are ready. Modifiers consume only already-owned usable resources. Accepted obligations follow recorded terms despite subsequent market-policy changes or death.

Carried inventory is limited; secure storage is separate. Funded delivery and accepted production reserve capacity. Exceptional recovery and estate obligations may use settlement-only overflow. Overflow cannot be equipped/traded/used as free general inventory; normal capacity shortage cannot destroy settled goods.

Final death immediately revokes active authority, records cause/rule versions and one estate, and freezes its cooldown decision. Resumable settlement handles secure goods, receivables and funded obligations; uncommitted orders use cancellation rules; carried goods use disclosed salvage/loss; guild property remains collective. Account cosmetics survive. There is no automatic inheritance or ghost gameplay. Existing public history preserves prior visibility, with no automatic obituary or private dossier.

Ordinary offline characters shelter. A committed encounter follows disclosed fallback and never uses app closure as instant escape. Every lethal transaction checks persistent freeze epoch; service failures pause lethal progression and adjust deadlines on recovery. No backlog of lethal timers may execute against unattended players. Valid deaths remain final. Proven invalid outcomes require evidence, causal reconciliation, two-person approval, audit and invariant verification.

## Initial content production budget

One region; three reviewed hubs; three standard gate templates (two solo, one party); eight enemy definitions; six archetypes (Warrior, Mage, Ranger, Assassin, Guardian, Healer), each three active abilities and one passive; 24 items; eight medicine/smithing/alchemy recipes; six ordinary NPCs and one finite rare encounter; 12 quests covering civilian/profession/merchant/hidden work; six dependable ordinary awakening routes; one registered fixed-price market plus separate intentional remote listings; one guild project and both founding paths. These are production limits, not completed content or permanent caps. Runebastion, Starcaller, Spatial Anchors and Courier Pacts remain illustrative content until authored and validated.

## Provisional service targets

At the defined 100-concurrent workload: ordinary API p95 <500 ms excluding external providers; consequential commands p95 <1 s outside deliberate contention; realtime propagation p95 <1 s after commit; reconnect snapshot <5 s after usable connectivity; 99% nonlethal jobs within 30 s; tactical deadline p99 within 2 s; zero unexplained economic discrepancies; rehearsed restore within 2 h; target RPO within 5 min using a suitable managed backup configuration; monthly availability target 99.5%. These are measurement objectives, not promises or achieved results.

Brief stages 0–5 map to blueprint phases: specification (0–1), playable foundation (2–7), multiplayer alpha foundation (8–13), regional mobile/lethal readiness and beta (14–19), expansion society (20), mature institutions/campaign (21). The blueprint additionally separates prototype, internal slice, multiplayer MVP, pre-alpha, closed alpha, regional beta and production evidence gates. Exact exit requirements remain in blueprint §§11 and 20. A browser screenshot is not multiplayer proof; simulation does not replace physical-device validation; no public lethal play before complete estates, succession, attribution, freezes and recovery drills.
